- On five to seven reading skills, the trained student is **above the
  panel's strongest member** (qwen3:14b, θ ≈ 2.35). Items much harder than
  b ≈ 2.5 cannot be calibrated by the current panel: when every member fails
  an item, its b is set mostly by the prior.
- So the work has two parts: **(1) generate much harder items** and
  **(2) extend the panel upward** so their difficulty can be measured.
- The Standard English skills (boundaries, form_structure_sense, transitions)
  are the student's weak skills, and the pool already covers its level there.

## Step 1a — stronger panel members (pilot)

- Candidates (Ollama on GPUs 3–7, no API cost): `qwen3:32b`, `llama3.3:70b`,
  `qwen2.5:72b` and `gpt-oss:120b` (fits one A100 80 GB). These families are
  already partly in the panel; the student (OLMo) is not.
- For each: score W (706 bank items) once, estimate θ per skill against the
  frozen item difficulties, and measure speed. Keep members with θ clearly
  above 2.35 on the reading skills that are fast enough for 3 passes over
  ~20k items.
- Any panel change re-estimates every item's b ("panel v2"). Confirmatory v1
  stays on panel v1; pool v2 is a new data version.

## Step 1b — hard-item generation (pilot)

Six high-θ skills × 2 strategies × 100 items ≈ 1,200 items (about $100):
- **G1:** the current D5 prompt, generated by Claude Opus 5.5 (stronger than
  the Opus 5 used so far).
- **G2 "beyond D5":** an adversarial prompt that asks for items strong
  reasoning models get wrong. It shows 3–5 of the pool's hardest items for
  the skill (b > 2) as style anchors and states what made them hard. It can
  feed back items the strong panel members solved.
- **Key quality first:** very hard items are the ones most likely to be
  wrong-keyed or ambiguous, and models may fail them for that reason.
  Require *two* independent blind verifications to agree with the key
  (Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5) and drop the rest.
- **Topic dedup:** drop any new item that twins an E1 or S item (bge-small
  cosine ≥ 0.88) or an existing pool item, to avoid worsening the topic-twin
  issue.
- Measure per strategy and skill: cost per item, key-agreement rate, and the
  share of items the strongest current members (qwen3:14b, deepseek-r1) and
  the step-1a candidates fail. That is the yield of "very hard".

## Step 2 — full build (after the pilot)

- Panel v2: the current 8 members + the chosen strong members. 3 passes on W,

- Generate with the better strategy until the high-θ skills have roughly
  +300 items with b(v2) > 2.0 each (target to be set from the pilot yield),
  then apply the double key verification and the topic dedup.
- New items enter **only the training pools (P/P+)**. S stays as is. For the
  held-out set, see decision 3.
- New split file and lock (`pool-v2`), new importer config.

## Cost and time (rough, to be replaced by pilot numbers)

- Generation with Opus 5.5 and adaptive thinking: about $0.06–0.10 per item;
  double verification about $0.03 per item. At a yield of 5–15% for
  b(v2) > 2, +2,000 very hard items need about 15–40k generated, i.e.
  $1.5–5k and 1–3 days at the Bedrock throughput (~30 items/min).
- Panel v2: about 20–40k items × 3 passes × (8 + 2–3) members on GPUs,
  roughly 3–6 days.
- Then the dev runs (~12 h) and v3 (~1.5 days).
